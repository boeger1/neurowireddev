Who Am i

I completed my degree in Computer Science in January 2025, and I’m now looking for new opportunities where I can grow and create meaningful digital solutions. I enjoy building things that make a real difference for people—whether it’s an app, a website, or a game. For me, programming isn’t just about writing code; it’s about understanding a problem, shaping an idea, and turning it into something useful.

Digital products help people in different ways. A simple app can make everyday tasks easier. A website can help a small business reach new customers. A game can entertain, inspire, or teach. No matter the format, I’m driven by the same goal: creating something that adds value.

Over time, I’ve learned that good software isn’t just functional—it’s thoughtful. It solves real problems, respects the user’s time, and feels intuitive to use. That’s the kind of work I want to be part of. I enjoy the process of exploring ideas, testing concepts, improving designs, and building solutions that feel both practical and enjoyable.
